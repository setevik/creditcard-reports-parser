Analyze Your Israeli Credit Card Reports Instantly

Credit Card Reports Parser is a free, browser-based tool that turns your raw credit card statements into a clear, categorized expense summary. Upload Excel or CSV files exported from Isracard (ישראכרט), Cal (כאל), or Max (מקס) and get an instant breakdown of your spending by vendor, category, date, and currency.

Whether you are tracking monthly household expenses, preparing financial reports, or just want a clearer picture of where your money goes, this tool does the heavy lifting. It automatically categorizes transactions into groups like Food, Supermarket, Transport, Shopping, Utilities, and more.

Your privacy comes first. All file processing happens entirely in your browser — no data is ever uploaded to a server. You can safely analyze sensitive financial information knowing it stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export my credit card report from Isracard?

Log in to the Isracard website (digital.isracard.co.il) or app. Go to Charge Details (פירוט חיובים), select the billing period you want, and click the Export to Excel button. Save the downloaded .xlsx file and upload it here.

How do I export my report from Cal (כאל)?

Log in to the Cal website (digital-web.cal-online.co.il) or app. Navigate to your card's Transaction Details (פירוט עסקאות), select the billing month, and click Export to Excel (ייצוא לאקסל). Save and upload the file.

How do I export my report from Max (מקס)?

Log in to the Max website (max.co.il) or app. Go to Charge Details (פירוט חיובים), select the billing month, and click Export to Excel (ייצוא לאקסל). Save and upload the file.

What file formats are supported?

The parser supports .xlsx, .xls, and .csv files exported from Isracard, Cal, and Max. You can upload reports from multiple cards and billing periods at the same time.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. All processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your credit card data is never uploaded to any server — it stays on your device. There is no backend, no database, and no tracking of your financial information.

What expense categories are available?

The tool auto-categorizes transactions into 17 categories: Food, Supermarket, Transport, Shopping, Utilities, Health/Pharma, Entertainment, Education, Insurance, Subscriptions, Government, Pets, Telecom, Fuel, and more. Unknown vendors are marked as "Other."

Can I upload reports from multiple cards at once?

Yes. You can upload multiple files from different cards and different billing periods. The parser will combine all transactions into a single summary, letting you see all your expenses in one place.
